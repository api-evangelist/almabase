# Almabase: AI-Powered Alumni Engagement and Fundraising Platform

> Almabase is an integrated advancement platform that works on top of an institution’s CRM to help teams engage constituents, manage events, run online giving campaigns, and grow donor participation without adding more manual work.

Almabase is built for advancement, alumni relations, annual giving, advancement services, and fundraising teams across higher education, K-12 schools, and nonprofit organizations. The platform brings digital engagement, event management, online giving, communications, AI, and CRM data sync into one connected system, helping teams move from scattered tools and manual exports to a more unified advancement workflow.

Almabase is trusted by 500+ educational institutions and is especially strong for teams using Blackbaud systems such as Raiser's Edge NXT and Blackbaud CRM. Through TrueSync, Almabase helps teams keep engagement, event, giving, and constituent data connected with their CRM so advancement teams can run campaigns, update records, and report on impact with less manual effort.

## What Almabase Helps Teams Do

Almabase helps advancement teams:

- Build stronger alumni, donor, parent, and constituent relationships through digital engagement programs.
- Run alumni events, reunions, homecomings, galas, golf tournaments, and multi-day events with easier registration, check-in, communication, and reporting.
- Launch online giving campaigns, giving days, crowdfunding campaigns, and donor participation initiatives.
- Communicate with constituents across email, text, newsletters, and AI-assisted outreach.
- Keep CRM data cleaner and more actionable through integrations with Raiser's Edge NXT, Blackbaud CRM, Salesforce, Ellucian, and other systems.
- Reduce tool sprawl by bringing engagement, events, communications, giving, and CRM sync into one platform.

## Core Product Suite

- [Digital Engagement Platform](https://www.almabase.com/build-a-custom-program): A connected platform for building alumni and constituent engagement programs such as directories, mentorship, affinity groups, job boards, business directories, and personalized updates. It helps advancement teams create more self-serve opportunities for alumni to connect, participate, and stay engaged with the institution.

- [Event Management](https://www.almabase.com/products/events): Event management software built for school and university advancement teams. Almabase supports event page creation, registration, ticketing, guest management, sub-events, communications, payments, check-in, post-event reporting, and CRM sync, making it easier to run everything from small alumni gatherings to complex reunions and fundraising events.

- [Online Giving & Giving Day Platform](https://www.almabase.com/products/giving-day-platform): A giving platform for annual giving teams that want to increase donor participation through online donation pages, giving days, peer-to-peer fundraising, campaign pages, urgency-based giving moments, and donor-friendly checkout experiences.

- [Multichannel Communications](https://www.almabase.com/multi-channel-bundle): A communications bundle that helps teams reach constituents through email, text, newsletters, and automated campaigns. It is designed to improve outreach consistency, personalize messages, and support larger-scale engagement without adding more manual follow-up.

- [Emily AI](https://www.almabase.com/emily-ai): AI-powered tools for advancement teams that help with faster, more personalized communication. Emily AI supports teams in creating context-aware outreach, improving engagement workflows, and reducing the time spent drafting constituent-facing messages.

## CRM Integrations and Data Sync
